🌿 About RePlate Intelligence

RePlate Intelligence is an AI-powered food waste management platform.
Predict Waste. Redistribute Food. Create Impact.

Aligned with UN SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption) and SDG 2 (Zero Hunger).
Backend: FastAPI + RandomForestRegressor · Database: SQLite · Frontend: Vanilla JS
